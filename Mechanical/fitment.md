# Fitment

Measured from the fit-test prints in `Gcode/testing/`.

## Fastener holes

The same sizes work for vertical and horizontal holes.

| Fastener | Clearance | Self-tap | Counterbore |
|---|---|---|---|
| M2 | 2.7 mm | 2.3 mm | 3.9 mm |
| M3 | 3.6 mm | 3.1 mm | 6.2 mm |
| M4 | 4.5 mm | 4.1 mm | 8.1 mm |

Where each size goes:

- **M4**: frame (self-tap, clearance, push-in nut), left and right mounts (clearance, push-in
  nut), left and right lids, bottom panel and base (clearance).
- **M3**: frame and left mount (self-tap).
- **M2**: frame (self-tap).
- **M5**: shooter (clearance).
