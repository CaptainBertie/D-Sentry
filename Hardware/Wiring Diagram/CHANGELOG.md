## Where the two "unknown" rows come from, and why they're not filled in further

A 2026-11-13 handoff checked a separate Claude.ai Project for an earlier version of these
diagrams and confirmed real prior history exists — just not retrievable the way we'd hoped:

- **Neither diagram was ever a Claude Artifact.** Artifacts keep their own revision history
  automatically; plain chat attachments don't. Both were Python-generated SVG/PNG files sent
  as ordinary file attachments, which means the only remaining copies are whatever's still in
  that chat's workspace or in the Mac's own Downloads folder — not something retrievable
  through the Artifact tool used for the rest of this handoff.
- The MOSFET-driver version came from a chat titled "Nerf turret wiring diagram".
- The L298N version came from a chat titled "L298N output voltage control", generated by a
  script that went through several named revisions (`gen.py`, `gen2.py`, `gen3.py`, plus a
  `gen3.bak.py` backup) — real edits, just not preserved as separate files anywhere git can
  see.
- A third, different diagram exists as an inline SVG (not a file, not an Artifact) in a chat
  called "Arduino power solutions with 9V battery", covering the UBEC/breadboard 5 V rail
  added in `../../DECISIONS.md` D20. It was never saved out, so it isn't here either.

**If you still have the actual old files** — pulled from that chat's downloads, or exported
from the script before it changed — add them under their real dates above and commit them as
their own revision, same as any other update (see `README.md`). Until then, this table is
the honest record: we know a MOSFET version and at least four script iterations of the L298N
version existed, we just can't produce the files from here.
